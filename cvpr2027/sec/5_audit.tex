Tang \etal~\cite{tang2020unbiased} release a causal MOTIFS PredCls checkpoint that yields two
models from one set of weights: the baseline (MOTIFS-SUM), whose predicate logit sums a
union-region visual term, a context term and a frequency prior indexed by the object pair, and
TDE, which subtracts the same sum with an averaged context and so keeps only the context term
minus its average. We ran it once with the authors' code on the canonical VG150 test split,
saving every branch's logits, and replayed the official evaluator offline (\cref{app:recall}).
The replay reproduces the official numbers to the fourth decimal: baseline $R@50=0.6612$ and
$mR@50=0.1459$, TDE $0.4588$ and $0.2476$; without the graph constraint TDE's mean recall
\emph{falls}, from $0.3260$ to $0.2981$. The audit covers $183{,}642$ relations over $26{,}446$
images in $7{,}127$ cells; $98.9\%$ fall in the $5{,}044$ cells holding at least two.

\paragraph{An operating-point control.} We add one model that needs no training: the
baseline's logits minus $\tau\log\pi(y)$, the training predicate prior, with
$\tau=1$~\cite{menon2021long}. Within a cell this adds the same constant to every
log-odds $\log q(y)-\log q(z)$, so it changes no within-cell ranking: whatever case-level
part it shows is existing discrimination spent at a new operating point, not new
discrimination. It reaches $mR@50=0.2343$ at $R@50=0.6437$. (It is not the inverse-frequency
scaling of logits that barely moves SGG mean recall~\cite{liu2025causal}.)

\paragraph{Protocol.} Unless a number says otherwise: cells are subject--object class pairs,
fixed by the benchmark's own object vocabulary before any outcome was seen; mean recall is
$mR@50$ with the graph constraint, micro-pooled over identified relations; intervals are
image-clustered; proper scores are matched by a temperature and a per-class bias per model,
fitted on half the test images; the AUC is relation-weighted. These two primary quantities
and the protocol were declared after the TDE and IETrans results were seen and before the DLFE
outputs existed, so for TDE and IETrans they are a post-hoc choice; every other protocol,
score and weighting is collected in \cref{tab:tdesummary}, where, with that many
configurations, one secondary interval excluding zero is not read as a finding.

\paragraph{Mean recall, split.} Pooled over identified relations, mean recall@50 rises by
$0.0972$ ($0.1017$ on the official per-image average). Of that, $+0.0841$ is group-level
and $+0.0130$ case-level, interval $[+0.0093,+0.0168]$ ($[+0.0088,+0.0173]$ by an image
jackknife; \cref{tab:sggaudit,app:clt}): $87\%$ of the
gain, interval $[83\%,91\%]$, survives shuffling every label among the relations that share
its object pair. The share is a property of the declared cells, not of TDE alone
(\cref{tab:sggaudit}, lower panel): refining the pair by the object's position or the subject's
size leaves it at $90\%$, the official per-image statistic gives $84\%$, but cells of the
subject class alone, which no longer hold the object's identity fixed, give $18\%$
(\cref{app:partition,app:splits}).
It lives in the body of the distribution: by training frequency (9 head predicates above
$10{,}000$ instances, 26 body, 15 tail below $500$), the body gains $+0.1224$, of which
$+0.1082$ is group-level, the head loses $0.0255$, and the tail gains $0.0003$ (the baseline
recalls no tail predicate; TDE recalls the predicate \textsf{to} at $0.016$). \Cref{fig:predicates} shows the mechanism one
predicate at a time. Recall of \textsf{parked on} rises from $0.000$ to $0.886$, almost all of
it group-level: TDE now says \textsf{parked on} for (\textsf{car}, \textsf{street}). Recall of \textsf{on} falls by $0.558$ in the same way.

\paragraph{The case-level part is matched by the control.} The control reproduces the
whole picture: $+0.0941$ of mean recall, $+0.0802$ group-level and $+0.0139$ case-level.
Against it, TDE's case-level part is $-0.0008$, interval $[-0.0050,+0.0033]$. When the
boundary between \textsf{on} and \textsf{parked on} moves, the cases that cross it are those
the baseline already scored as more \textsf{parked on}-like, and they are more often labelled
so: the covariance is real, but bought with discrimination the baseline had. The direct test
is the within-cell AUC, which no prior shift or recalibration can move: the control's
change is below $10^{-6}$. Weighted as the split weights cells, the primary weighting, TDE's
change is $-0.0018$, $[-0.0043,+0.0016]$; by comparison $+0.0025$, $[-0.0028,+0.0087]$. Both
put most weight on head predicates ($57\%$ and $65\%$ on head--head pairs), so we add two that
do not: every predicate weighted equally, as mean recall weights them, and every predicate
pair weighted equally (\cref{app:rankrob}). With $1{,}000$ image-bootstrap replicates the
$90\%$ intervals exclude a change larger than $0.0038$ under the primary weighting, $0.0077$
by comparison and $0.028$ per predicate; per pair TDE's AUC \emph{falls}, $-0.0153$,
$[-0.0249,-0.0004]$. For scale, box geometry buys $0.063$, $0.032$ and $0.069$ over the class
pair under the first three (\cref{sec:vgvisual}): the bound is under a tenth of that under the
primary weighting, a quarter by comparison and two fifths per predicate, where tail--tail
comparisons are too few to decide ($-0.097$, $[-0.228,+0.024]$). No weighting shows TDE gaining
discrimination, though the baseline's AUC ($0.61$, $0.57$) left room; the margins were set
after TDE's results were seen. Against the control
the mean-recall case-level part excludes only changes above $0.0043$, a third of TDE's own,
too wide to call it zero.

\paragraph{The proper score, matched.} Under the primary protocol (\cref{sec:matching}),
which makes the control identical to the baseline, TDE's quadratic-score change is
$-0.00951$: $-0.01396$ group-level and $+0.00445$, $[+0.00354,+0.00536]$, $p=.001$, case-level
(\cref{fig:where}; cross-fitted $+0.00399$). That part is not a ranking measure: removing
TDE's frequency prior, which leaves every within-cell ranking as it was (AUC change
$-0.00001$), gives $+0.00817$, $[+0.00757,+0.00877]$, under the same protocol, since a per-class
bias cannot absorb a shift that differs from pair to pair; ranking is read from the AUC
alone. The protocol also decides the sign: with a temperature alone the part is
$-0.00006$, $[-0.00120,+0.00109]$ ($p=.555$), and with one temperature shared by both models $-0.0184$;
over 20 calibration halves the primary part stays positive, and merging synonymous predicates
changes none of this (\cref{app:rankrob,app:splits,app:merge}).

\paragraph{What that licenses.} On this checkpoint the ten points of mean recall are a change in
which predicates the model favours within each object pair. A one-line prior shift that ranks
the relations of every cell exactly as the baseline did reproduces the gain, its split and
its tier profile at under a tenth of TDE's cost in $R@50$ ($0.0175$ against $0.2023$), and a
better $F@50$, the harmonic mean of $R@50$ and $mR@50$~\cite{zhang2022finegrained} ($0.344$ against $0.322$). \Cref{tab:protocols} gives all six recall protocols: the share runs from $77\%$ at
$K=20$ to $86\%$ at $K=100$ under the graph constraint; without it TDE's mean recall falls,
and its case-level part over the control turns positive at $K=100$, $+0.0148$,
$[+0.0064,+0.0232]$. The control's match of TDE's gain is specific to $K=50$ under the
constraint: TDE leads it by $+0.0300$ at $K=100$ and trails it by $0.0234$ at $K=20$ and by
$0.08$--$0.15$ without the constraint. Where the total gain is negative, as without the
constraint, a share is undefined and we report the parts only.

\paragraph{How to read the split.} Group-level is not a lesser gain: retrieval by predicate
wants rare predicates named more often for a pair, and mean recall rewards that by design;
captioning or question answering about one image wants the right predicate on the right
relation, a case-level gain. The case-level part also credits shortcuts, operating-point
movement and label noise, so discrimination claims rest on the control and the AUC; an AUC
gain beyond the bounds above would change TDE's verdict. Nor is the share forced by
the metric: an oracle gains $+0.5068$ in quadratic score, all case-level ($+0.8041$ against
$-0.2973$). And it depends on the label mix: along a path to uniform predicate frequencies
within each pair, TDE's temperature-matched quadratic case-level part rises from $-0.00006$ to $+0.01430$
while its AUC change stays undetected (\cref{sec:vgvisual}).

\paragraph{Why.} TDE's change is three subtractions whose splits add up exactly
(\cref{app:tdepath}); the averaged context supplies $+0.0817$ of the $0.0972$, and that term is,
to $99.96\%$ of its energy, one vector shared by every relation, correlated $+0.75$ with the log
training prior: in PredCls TDE is to first order a learned logit adjustment. On zero-shot
recall, where TDE beats the control by $+0.035$, its case-level part, $+0.013$, comes with an AUC
that \emph{falls} on comparisons involving an unseen triplet, $-0.015$, $[-0.020,-0.006]$
(\cref{app:zeroshot}).

\paragraph{Two more methods.} DLFE~\cite{chiou2021recovering} divides predicate
probabilities by estimated label frequencies, a per-predicate log-odds shift, and is released
with the baseline it was trained against, so this comparison is controlled (\cref{app:dlfe}).
Its $mR@50$ gain, $+0.1112$, is $+0.1046$ group-level and $+0.0065$, $[+0.0010,+0.0120]$,
case-level; the relation-weighted AUC gains $+0.0029$, $[+0.0001,+0.0055]$, a twentieth of
what box geometry buys, and no other weighting detects a change (\cref{app:dlfe}). IETrans~\cite{zhang2022finegrained} relabels training data from
general to specific predicates within an object pair and adds the training log-prior at test
time; its released Neural-Motifs PredCls checkpoint is the IETrans+Rwt variant, also trained
with a reweighted loss, re-scored the same way (\cref{app:ietrans}). It reaches $mR@50=0.3587$:
$+0.2204$ over the baseline, $+0.2143$ group-level and $+0.0060$, $[-0.0031,+0.0151]$,
case-level. Its relation-weighted AUC falls by $-0.0238$, $[-0.0272,-0.0187]$, and by
comparison barely, $-0.0093$, $[-0.0185,-0.0001]$. A fall measures agreement with
the recorded labels, and IETrans relabels general predicates as specific ones, so part of it
may be synonymy rather than lost discrimination. No IETrans baseline was released: this
compares two separately trained models and does not isolate the effect of relabelling; merging
synonymous predicates leaves the loss in place (\cref{app:ambiguity}).

\paragraph{A method that is not a prior correction.} PE-Net~\cite{zheng2023prototype} scores a
relation by its similarity to learned predicate prototypes, with no prior. Its released PredCls checkpoint, re-run and replayed like the others ($R@50=0.6523$,
$mR@50=0.3142$, as published; \cref{app:penet}), gains $+0.1703$ of mean recall over the
separately trained MOTIFS baseline: $+0.1460$ group-level ($86\%$) and $+0.0243$,
$[+0.0197,+0.0289]$, case-level. Unlike TDE's, its within-cell AUC \emph{rises}, $+0.0050$,
$[+0.0008,+0.0091]$, relation-weighted and $+0.0187$, $[+0.0037,+0.0337]$, per predicate pair,
and matched, its quadratic gain is all case-level ($+0.00609$ of $+0.00634$). Where a method
does gain discrimination, the split shows it, here a twelfth of what box geometry buys.

\paragraph{Beyond PredCls.} The release's SGCls and SGDet checkpoints give all three
models shared object predictions, so the ground-truth pair remains a common grouping
(\cref{app:det}). In SGCls, where $58\%$ of relations have a matched pair, TDE raises $mR@50$
by $+0.0517$, $99\%$ group-level; the control gains $+0.0518$ at $R@50=0.384$ against TDE's
$0.263$; TDE's relation-weighted AUC falls by $-0.0128$, $[-0.0171,-0.0074]$. In SGDet ($65\%$ matched;
\cref{app:det}) the control again gains more ($+0.0418$ against $+0.0310$).
