Tang \etal~\cite{tang2020unbiased} release a causal MOTIFS PredCls checkpoint that yields two
models from one set of weights: the baseline (MOTIFS-SUM) and its Total Direct Effect
counterpart (MOTIFS-TDE). The baseline's predicate logit is a sum of three terms: a visual
term from the union region, a context term, and a frequency prior indexed by the object pair,
which in PredCls is fixed by the subject--object cell. TDE subtracts the same sum evaluated
with an averaged context. The visual and frequency terms enter both halves unchanged and
cancel, so TDE's output is the context term minus its averaged counterpart: it removes the
frequency prior, constant within a cell, and also the image-specific visual term, which is
not. We ran the released checkpoint once with the authors' code on the canonical VG150 test
split, saving every branch's logits for every object pair, and re-scored them offline with
an exact replay of the official evaluator's matching (\cref{app:recall}). The replay
reproduces the official numbers to the fourth decimal: the baseline reaches $R@50=0.6612$
with $mR@50=0.1459$, TDE reaches $mR@50=0.2476$ while $R@50$ falls to $0.4588$, and without
the graph constraint TDE's mean recall \emph{falls}, from $0.3260$ to $0.2981$. The audit
covers $183{,}642$ relations over $26{,}446$ images in $7{,}127$ subject--object cells;
$98.9\%$ of the relations fall in the $5{,}044$ cells holding at least two.

\paragraph{An operating-point control.} We add one model that needs no training: the
baseline's logits minus $\tau\log\pi(y)$, the training predicate prior, with
$\tau=1$~\cite{menon2021long}. Within a cell this adds the same constant to every
log-odds $\log q(y)-\log q(z)$, so it changes no within-cell ranking: whatever case-level
part it shows is existing discrimination spent at a new operating point, not new
discrimination. It reaches $mR@50=0.2343$ at $R@50=0.6437$.

\paragraph{Mean recall, split.} Pooled over identified relations, mean recall@50 rises by
$0.0972$ ($0.1017$ on the official per-image average). Of that, $+0.0841$ is group-level
and $+0.0130$ case-level, interval $[+0.0093,+0.0168]$ (\cref{tab:sggaudit}): $87\%$ of the
gain survives shuffling every label among the relations that share its object pair.
It lives in the body of the distribution. Splitting predicates by training frequency (9
head above $10{,}000$ instances, 26 body, 15 tail below $500$), the body gains $+0.1224$, of
which $+0.1082$ is group-level; the head loses $0.0255$; the tail gains $0.0003$. Of the 15 tail
predicates, $1{,}480$ test relations, the baseline recalls none and TDE recalls one,
\textsf{to}, at $0.016$: none of the ten points comes from the rarest predicates. \Cref{fig:predicates} shows the mechanism one
predicate at a time. Recall of \textsf{parked on} rises from $0.000$ to $0.886$, almost all of
it group-level: TDE now says \textsf{parked on} for (\textsf{car}, \textsf{street}), and so
recalls most relations that carry it. Recall of \textsf{on} falls by $0.558$ in the same way.

\paragraph{The case-level part is matched by the control.} The control reproduces the
whole picture: $+0.0941$ of mean recall, $+0.0802$ group-level and $+0.0139$ case-level.
Against it, TDE's case-level part is $-0.0008$, interval $[-0.0050,+0.0033]$. So a
case-level part of a thresholded metric is not by itself evidence of new discrimination.
When the boundary between \textsf{on} and \textsf{parked on} moves, the cases that cross it
are those the baseline already scored as more \textsf{parked on}-like, and they are more
often labelled so; the covariance of hit gains with labels is real, but it was bought with
discrimination the baseline had. The direct test is the within-cell AUC, which no prior
shift or recalibration can move: TDE's change is $+0.0026$, interval $[-0.0028,+0.0086]$.

\paragraph{The proper score agrees.} With each model's temperature fitted on held-out
images ($T^*=2.50$ baseline, $0.92$ TDE), TDE's quadratic-score change is $-0.18264$, of
which $-0.18258$ is group-level; the case-level part is $-0.00006$, interval
$[-0.00120,+0.00109]$ (\cref{fig:where}). Under the log score it is $+0.04396$ against a
group-level $-0.59157$. Against the control the two scores disagree in sign ($-0.0040$
quadratic, $+0.0154$ log, both intervals excluding zero), and the control's own case-level
parts ($+0.0039$, $+0.0285$) show that the proper-score split, too, responds to the
operating point (\cref{app:recall}). Unmatched outputs and a coarser grouping give different
answers; all configurations are in \cref{app:recall}.

\paragraph{What that licenses.} On this checkpoint, the ten points of mean recall are a change
in which predicates the model favours within each object pair. Everything they did --- the
gain, its split, its tier profile --- a one-line prior shift that ranks the relations of
every cell exactly as the baseline did also does, at under a tenth of TDE's cost in $R@50$ ($0.0175$ against $0.2023$). Measured
against that control, neither recall nor the threshold-free AUC finds any within-cell
discrimination TDE gained, and the two proper scores split on its sign. That is not built into TDE.
Its subtraction removes the image-specific visual term along with the prior; a case-level
change near zero means whatever it lost there it recovered elsewhere, or neither moved.
