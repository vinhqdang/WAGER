\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations.} Six bound what the split can say. \emph{It is conditional on the grouping}:
two equally fine partitions of the same cases can disagree about whether a gain is case-level
(\cref{app:partition}), so declaring $\phi$ is a modelling decision the benchmark should make
before a leaderboard opens: refining the class pair by position or size leaves the share at
$90\%$, dropping the object class takes it to $18\%$. \emph{It credits any within-cell signal}, shortcuts included: an
unrecorded covariate correlated at $0.061$ with both parts would account for the entire
case-level gain of \cref{sec:data} (\cref{app:sensitivity}). \emph{It is not invariant to
recalibration, the operating point or the label mix}, so we report two matching protocols; the
within-cell AUC, invariant to the first two, gives up exact additivity. \emph{Its interval
assumes cells that are not tiny}: with every cell holding two cases it covers $81.5\%$, from
three up it is nominal (\cref{app:clt}), and $1.0\%$ of audited relations sit in two-case
cells. And \emph{it needs scores for every label}, which most benchmarks do not archive;
recovering them took a rerun of the released checkpoint. \emph{Each released model is one
training run}: the intervals cover test sampling, not training variability, and the matching is
fitted on half of the test images, as no validation outputs were released.

\paragraph{Conclusion.} A ten-point mean-recall gain is two achievements summed, and mean
recall reports the sum; scoring each prediction against another case's label from the
same group separates them exactly. On two released Neural-Motifs checkpoints on Visual Genome
the gains are almost entirely group-level: TDE's is matched by a ranking-preserving logit
adjustment and its within-group ranking moves by less than $0.0034$ under the split's
weighting; IETrans's, against a separately trained baseline, lowers it; we audit no later method. For such benchmarks we
suggest reporting, beside the headline metric, the declared grouping with its cell sizes, both
parts with intervals under the matching protocols, the within-group AUC under both weightings
with the change each excludes, and, for a thresholded metric, the operating-point control.
