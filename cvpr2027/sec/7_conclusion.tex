\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Limitations.} Five bound what the split can say. \emph{It is conditional on the grouping.} The case-level part measures
discrimination among relations sharing an object pair; two equally fine partitions of the
same cases can disagree completely about whether a gain is case-level
(\cref{app:partition}), so declaring $\phi$ is a modelling decision that the benchmark, not
the submitting author, should make before a leaderboard opens. \emph{It credits any
within-cell signal}, including shortcuts nobody recorded, and our own bound on that exposure is
not reassuring: for the controlled-predictor pair of \cref{sec:data}, an unrecorded covariate
correlated at $0.061$ with both parts would account for the entire case-level gain
(\cref{app:sensitivity}). \emph{It is not invariant to recalibration, to the operating point or to the label mix}: a
temperature does not undo a class-wise shift of the operating point (\cref{sec:simulation}),
so we report matching with a temperature and with a per-class bias as well; the within-cell
AUC, invariant to both, gives up exact additivity; and a case-level null can be a
cancellation that another label mix undoes (\cref{sec:vgvisual}). \emph{Its interval assumes
cells that are not tiny}: with every cell holding two cases it covers $81.5\%$, not $95\%$, and
from three cases up it reaches nominal coverage (\cref{app:clt}); $1.0\%$ of VG150's audited
relations sit in two-case cells. And \emph{it needs
scores for every label}: most benchmarks archive only ranked top-$k$ predictions, and
recovering the rest took a rerun of the released checkpoint: that, not compute, blocks routine use.

\paragraph{Conclusion.} A ten-point mean-recall gain is two achievements summed, and the
field's metrics report the sum. Scoring each prediction against another case's label from the
same group separates them exactly. On two released debiasing methods the gains are almost
entirely group-level: TDE's is matched by a ranking-preserving logit adjustment and leaves
within-group ranking unchanged as far as we can detect; IETrans's costs within-group
discrimination. We suggest that benchmarks whose labels lean on a feature every model sees
report, beside the headline metric, the declared grouping with its coverage and cell sizes,
both signed parts with intervals under both matching protocols, the within-group AUC under
both weightings, the parts along a path of label mixes, and, for a thresholded metric, the
operating-point control; each model's within-group reliance (\cref{prop:reliance}) is a
one-column summary a leaderboard can carry. Code and results are in the supplement.
