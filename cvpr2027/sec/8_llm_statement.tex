\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
Large language models were used extensively in this work, under the author's direction and
review. Claude models (Anthropic; Opus, Sonnet and Haiku, through the Claude Code agent)
wrote most of the analysis and experiment code (the decomposition and AUC library, the
replay of the official evaluator, the Colab pipelines that re-ran the released checkpoints,
the audit scripts and the script that checks every number in the manuscript against the
stored results), ran the experiments and analyses, drafted and revised the text, tables and
figures of the paper and the supplementary material, searched and checked the related
literature, and produced simulated peer reviews that guided revisions. Every reported number
is produced by code from stored model outputs and checked automatically against the text, and
every reference was checked against its published source. The author is responsible for all
content.
