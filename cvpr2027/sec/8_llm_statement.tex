\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
Large language models (Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku, Anthropic, used through the Claude Code
agent) supported the writing and wrote code. For the writing, they drafted and edited text,
tables and figure layouts of the paper and the supplementary material, helped search and check
the related literature and references, and produced simulated reviews used as feedback. For the
code, they wrote and ran the analysis library, the replay of the official evaluator, the
pipelines that re-ran the released checkpoints, the audit scripts and the script that checks
every number in the manuscript against the stored results. The author reviewed and edited all
text and code, checked the results, and takes full responsibility for the content.
