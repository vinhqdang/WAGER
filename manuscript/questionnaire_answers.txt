Q3. Novelty of the paper (limit 500 chars)
-------------------------------------------
[500/500]  PASTE THIS:
Comparing two fitted predictors, the score difference confounds a closer fit to a known nuisance feature with better prediction of individual cases. We give an exact finite-sample identity separating them by within-cell label transport, needing no fitted nuisance model and no tuning parameter. The residual is an order-two U-statistic equal to a within-cell covariance gain, identifying it with the resolution term of classical proper-score decomposition applied to a model pair, not one forecaster.


Q4. Main contribution of the paper (limit 1000 chars)
------------------------------------------------------
[996/1000]  PASTE THIS:
An estimator, with theory and inference, attributing the proper-score difference between two frozen probabilistic models into a nuisance-transported component and an instance-alignment residual. Contributions: (i) an exact population and finite-sample identity requiring no fitted nuisance model, no sample splitting, no tuning parameter, computable in O(NK); (ii) a covariance identity showing the residual is the resolution term of the classical decomposition applied to a model pair; (iii) two exact results, that leave-one-out transport removes an (n_c-1)/n_c attenuation the in-sample plug-in incurs, and that coarsening the matching feature shifts the estimand by an explicit between-cell covariance; (iv) Hajek influence-function inference with cluster-robust intervals, coverage-validated by simulation; (v) discriminant-validity experiments establishing what the components do and do not separate, including sensitivity to monotone recalibration and the matched protocol that removes it.
