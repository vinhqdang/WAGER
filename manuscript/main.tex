\section*{Supplementary material}
Proofs of all numbered results, the influence-function derivation, implementation and
reproducibility details, two further empirical studies (long-tailed image and text
classification) and the granularity, score and minimum-cell-size sensitivity analyses are
provided as supplementary material, whose sections, tables and figures carry an
\textsf{S} prefix and are cited in that form throughout.

\section*{Data and code availability}
All code, cached model outputs and scripts needed to reproduce every number, table and
figure are publicly available at \url{https://github.com/vinhqdang/WAGER}: the estimator,
its unit tests, every experiment driver, and the cached results each reported value is
checked against by an included verification script. The two audited scene-graph checkpoints
are the public release of \citet{tang2020unbiased}; Visual Genome
\citep{krishna2017visual} and CIFAR-100 are public benchmarks.
