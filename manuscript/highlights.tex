\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent WAGER: An Exact Decomposition of Model-Pair Score Gains on Benchmarks with Strong
Label Priors

\vspace{1em}
\begin{itemize}
\item Exact split of a model-pair score gain into label-prior fit and case-level evidence
\item Audits released scene-graph checkpoints: a 10-point mean-recall gain is prior-fit
\item Runs in O(NK) on cached predictions; no baseline model, fold or tuning parameter
\item Cluster-robust intervals, a limit theorem, and a group-stratified permutation test
\item A CLIP model losing on every aggregate metric is shown to discriminate cases better
\end{itemize}
