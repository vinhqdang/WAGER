We introduce WAGER (Wealth-Anchored Gain Estimation of Reasoning), a distribution-free, anytime-valid framework that decomposes a reported gain on computer-vision benchmarks into a prior-recoverable part and a genuine visual-reasoning part. Instead of comparing a model to an external frequency baseline, WAGER constructs a self-prior projection—the model's own predictions averaged within frequency-defining cells—and runs a testing-by-betting capital process. The growth rate of this process quantifies usable reasoning information beyond the prior, and the derived Reasoning Gain Ratio (RGR) provides a confidence interval for the substantive gain. We evaluate SGG, VQA, and LVIS models without requiring model retraining or complex distributional assumptions.
