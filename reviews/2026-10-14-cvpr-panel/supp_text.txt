         D. Coarsening and unrecorded covariates                                                                                                    988

         Appendix N.4 reports that replacing the class-pair cell with a coarser subject-only cell changes the estimated covariance                  989
         gain. The following result gives that sensitivity an exact population-level mechanism rather than treating it as an empirical              990
         curiosity.                                                                                                                                 991
            The first term is the coarse cell’s share of the fine-grained covariance gain; the second is a between-cell covariance, over            992
         the finer cells nested inside c̄, between each fine cell’s typical gain at label y and its label-y frequency. It is not sign definite,     993
         so coarsening ϕ can inflate or deflate the credited covariance gain, and it vanishes exactly when either quantity is constant              994
         within each coarse cell. This is why the finest defensible ϕ is the conservative default: coarsening adds cross-cell prior                 995
         structure into ∆R, while the finer partition’s gain is preserved on average through the first term. The proof is a per-label               996
         application of the law of total covariance (Appendix E).                                                                                   997

1304     A recalibration-invariant alternative, and what it would cost. Because ∆R is a covariance between forecast values, its
1305     sensitivity to recalibration is structural rather than incidental, and a reader may reasonably ask why we do not use a rank-
1306     based within-cell contrast instead — a within-cell paired AUC difference, say, for which DeLong et al. [10] already supply
1307     the correlated-U-statistic variance. Such a quantity would be invariant to any monotone rescaling of either model and would
1308     dissolve the confound this paper spends a protocol on. We think the trade is worth stating and is not obviously in our favour
1309     on every axis. What a rank-based contrast gives up is the exact identity: it is not a component of the score difference, so
1310     it cannot be added to anything to recover ∆T , and a benchmark reporting it learns how the models rank cases but not how
1311     the reported score gain decomposes. The construction here buys exact additivity with respect to a declared proper score, and
1312     pays for it with scale dependence. A rank-based diagnostic is the natural companion rather than a competitor, and reporting
1313     both — one exact and scale-dependent, one invariant and non-additive — is what we would now recommend to a benchmark
1314     that could choose.

1332     L. Terminology
1333     M. Why the transported gain is not prior fit
1334     Proposition 1 splits ∆Pc into two brackets, and only the first is prior fit: it is positive exactly when the new model’s average
1335     within-cell forecast sits closer to the cell’s label frequencies. The second has nothing to do with the prior. It rewards the
1336     new model for making less dispersed predictions inside the cell, at a fixed mean forecast, so ∆P can be large and positive
1337     when neither model’s prior fit has changed at all. One cell suffices: with two labels and frequencies ( 21 , 12 ), let q0 alternate
1338     between (1, 0) and (0, 1) in a pattern uncorrelated with the label and let q1 predict ( 12 , 12 ) throughout. Both mean forecasts
1339     equal the cell frequencies exactly, so the first bracket is zero, and the whole observed gain of 12 is transported, produced
1340     entirely by the collapse in dispersion. The channel is therefore the reliability-and-sharpness channel of the classical partition,
1341     not a prior-fitting channel, which is why we name it for the operation. Two consequences: a claim that some gain is “mostly
1342     prior-fitting” does not follow from a large ∆Pd alone, and Eq. (8) explains why confidence matching moves ∆P       d so sharply,
1343     since a temperature change is almost purely a change in dispersion.

1402     N.3. Real pixels against a geometric proxy, in full
1403     None of the predictors above ever sees an image: they are functions of the class pair and, for MLP-SPATIAL(+), of box
1404     coordinates recorded in the annotation. Since box geometry is only a proxy for image content, a sceptical reader may ask
1405     whether the covariance channel responds to pixel evidence or merely to that proxy. Figure 5 sharpens the question — the cell
1406     ϕ = (man, surfboard) holds both “riding” and “carrying”, and what distinguishes them is what the photograph shows.
1407         MLP-VISUAL answers it. Subject and object regions are cropped from the original image and each encoded by a frozen
1408     CLIP ViT-B/32 encoder [34]; the two 512-dimensional embeddings are concatenated with the class embeddings MLP-CLASS
1409     already uses and passed to a small trained head. Because CLIP is never fine-tuned, whatever covariance the model gains
1410     reflects information its pretraining already recovered from pixels rather than representation learning on Visual Genome.
1411         Encoding every training relation with CLIP is expensive, so MLP-VISUAL must be trained on a subsample; setting a
1412     subsampled model against the full-data MLP-SPATIAL would confound feature content with training-set size. All three
1413     compared predictors are therefore retrained on one fixed, seeded subsample of 100,000 of the 532,987 training relations,
1414     written MLP-CLASS-S, MLP-SPATIAL-S and MLP-VISUAL-S, so any gap between them isolates the features. The audit
1415     still uses the full 229,605-relation test split with the same class-pair ϕ and image-clustered intervals.

1416     Result. By any aggregate measure the CLIP model is the worst of the three: top-1 accuracy 0.6156 against 0.6467 for
1417     MLP-SPATIAL-S and 0.6416 for MLP-CLASS-S, and a quadratic score 0.04642 below MLP-SPATIAL-S. An evaluator
1418     reading either number would discard it.
1419        The decomposition contradicts that reading on the axis that matters. Against MLP-SPATIAL-S the CLIP model loses
1420     0.05282 of transported gain while gaining 0.00640 of covariance (95% CI [0.00513, 0.00767], randomization p = .002): its
1421     entire deficit sits in the transported channel and its within-cell discrimination is significantly better, not worse. Against the
1422     class-only baseline the point is plainer still — pixels buy 0.01663 of covariance where box geometry buys 0.01023, with
1423     well-separated intervals. Real image content carries more case-specific signal than the geometric proxy standing in for it.
1424        A plausible mechanism, stated as hypothesis rather than finding: the 1024 CLIP dimensions dominate the 64 dimensions
1425     of class embedding the head also receives, so the model fits VG150’s class-pair frequency structure markedly less well than
1426     a predictor built around it. A benchmark whose headline metric rewards transported gain will punish this model, and the
1427     aggregate numbers duly do. What the decomposition adds is that the punishment is not for failing to use the image. Two
1428     falsification checks, reported in Section H, support that reading: correcting a model’s within-cell prior towards the training
1429     histogram registers as almost pure transported movement (+0.02390 against +0.00114 of covariance), as a correction carry-
1430     ing no case-level information must, and applied to the CLIP model it halves the aggregate deficit (−0.04642 to −0.02138)
1431     while the covariance advantage persists (+0.00754, and +0.00664 when both models are corrected) and survives confidence
1432     matching (+0.00464, CI [+0.00311, +0.00618]).

1452     N.5. Long-tailed image classification
1453     Sections 4–6 are all instances of one task (predicate classification on an ordered object pair). Section 2 argued that long-tailed
1454     image classification poses a structurally identical question: a class-frequency prior can be fit better without any per-instance
1455     improvement. We test this directly on CIFAR-100-LT [8], an exponential-imbalance (ratio 100) subsample of CIFAR-100’s
1456     training split with the original, balanced test split retained for evaluation. We train ResNet-32 [17] classifiers that differ only
1457     in their per-class loss weighting: a vanilla cross-entropy baseline (CE); class-balanced effective-number re-weighting applied
1458     from initialization (CB) [8]; and the same re-weighting deferred to the final twenty epochs (DRW) [3]. Optimizer, schedule,
1459     augmentation, epoch budget, and seed are identical across runs (Section H).

1460     Choosing ϕ. A declared grouping variable has to predict the label without determining it, and this second benchmark makes
1461     the constraint unusually easy to violate. Were ϕ taken to be the fine class label itself, every cell would be label-homogeneous,
1462     within-cell transport would have nothing to cross, and ∆R d would come out identically zero – an algebraic artifact wearing the
1463     appearance of a finding. CIFAR-100’s own 20 coarse superclasses avoid this, each gathering five fine labels into a cell of 500
1464     balanced test images, which is structurally the situation of a VG class-pair cell holding several predicates (Figure 5). Unlike a
1465     class pair, though, a superclass is a function of the label, not of the input, and no model observes it at test time: the superclass
1466     rows show how the estimator behaves under a label-derived grouping, not an audit of a feature both models condition on.
1467     The input-grounded row is the global cell, which every model trivially observes. Alongside the superclass audit we report
1468     two further declared priors: the trivial coarsening to a single global cell, which audits against the global class marginal and so
1469     instantiates Proposition 3 in a second domain, and a partition into head, medium and tail training-frequency tiers. CIFAR-100
1470     test images are independent, unlike the several relations VG draws from one photograph, so no cluster grouping is required
1471     and the plain Hájek interval of Section 3 applies directly. One semantic point matters in this domain: transport uses each
1472     cell’s test label histogram, which on CIFAR-100-LT is balanced. A positive ∆P here therefore records movement toward
1473     the balanced test marginal – undoing the training-frequency skew – which is precisely the adjustment long-tailed evaluation
1474     is designed to reward; a transported gain is a description of where score was earned, not an accusation (Section 7).

1475     Result. The three classifiers reach top-1 accuracies of 0.3662 (CE), 0.2627 (CB) and 0.3874 (DRW), so re-weighting from
1476     initialization degrades the classifier substantially while the deferred schedule delivers the improvement it was designed to
1477     produce. Table 13 decomposes both gains against the superclass audit.
1478         Of the two, the class-balanced run is the more instructive, precisely because its aggregate score conceals almost everything
1479     of interest. On the quadratic score it gains +0.00143 over the baseline, a difference small enough that a reader working from
1480     the headline number alone would reasonably conclude that re-weighting had changed very little. The decomposition tells
1481     another story: within superclasses the transported channel improves by +0.19713 while within-group covariance deteriorates
1482     by −0.19569 (95% CI [−0.20728, −0.18411]), so the near-zero total is not a small effect but two large ones that happen to
1483     cancel.

1484     A calibration-matched regime. A channel split of that shape could, however, be produced by confidence scaling alone.
1485     Under the quadratic score the covariance channel is a covariance between probability movements and labels (Eq. (3)), and
1486     a monotone recalibration moves it: softening an overconfident model transfers score mass from the covariance channel to
1487     the transported channel while adding no information about any individual image. The baseline invites exactly this concern,
1488     assigning a mean maximum probability of 0.764 while being correct on only 0.3662 of the test set. We therefore repeat the
1489     audit in a calibration-matched regime: each model’s temperature is chosen by held-out likelihood on a calibration half of the
1490     test split (T ∗ = 2.85 for CE, 3.51 for CB, 2.61 for DRW), the decomposition is computed on the disjoint audit half, and the
1491     whole procedure is repeated over twenty random splits (Table 11). The regime’s necessity is quantified by its control row:
1492     recalibrating the baseline alone – a transformation carrying no instance information – moves the channels by +0.380 (prior)

         O.1. TDE along its branches                                                                                                         1640

         In PredCls the baseline’s logits are vis + ctx + frq and TDE’s are ctx − ctxavg , so the change runs through two intermediate       1641
         models built from the same branches: the baseline without the frequency prior (vis + ctx), and the context term alone. The          1642
         split is linear in the gain vector, the AUC is a difference of per-model values, and in the matched proper-score split each         1643
         model keeps one held-out temperature throughout; so along this path every part adds up to TDE’s exactly, which the script           1644
         asserts (Tab. 22). The order of the three steps is a choice, and the steps are attributions along it, not order-free effects.       1645

             Two observations make the mechanism plain. Dropping the frequency prior, which is constant within every cell, cannot            1646
         move the within-cell AUC and does not; it moves mean recall by −0.0009, but shifts the matched quadratic case-level part            1647
         by +0.00915, another instance of the operating-point effect. And the averaged-context term that TDE subtracts is almost a           1648
         constant: splitting each logit term’s energy, after removing each row’s mean, into the vector shared by every relation, what        1649
         the subject–object cell adds, and what varies within cells (Tab. 23), 99.96% of ctxavg is the shared vector, with a non-shared      1650
         remainder of 0.021 logits against a size of 1.10. The shared vector correlates +0.75 with the log training prior. Subtracting it    1651
         is therefore, to first order, a logit adjustment of the context branch by a learned prior; its small remainder is what moves the    1652
         within-cell AUC by +0.0016.                                                                                                         1653

         Q.3. A label-shifted test                                                                                                                   1760
                                                                        P
         Section 3 writes a cell’s case-level part as ∆Rc =           y<z p(y)p(z) D(y, z), where D(y, z) depends only on the class-                 1761
         conditional behaviour of the gain. A label shift that leaves P (X | Y, C) fixed changes the weights p(y)p(z) and no D, while                1762
         the group-level part depends on the cell’s label frequencies directly. So a case-level part keeps its sign under every such shift           1763
         if its D(y, z) share one sign, and can change sign otherwise; that much follows from the identity, and the reweighted estimator             1764
         below inherits it. What the data decide is which comparisons have D(y, z) of mixed sign, and how far the group-level parts                  1765
         move. This is a sensitivity analysis, not an independent test.                                                                              1766
             Relation i in cell c gets weight wi = 1/(Lc p̂c (yi )), with Lc the number of predicates present in the cell and p̂c their              1767
         frequencies, so that every predicate present in a cell carries equal total weight; the path wiα , α ∈ {0, 41 , 12 , 34 , 1}, moves from     1768
         the benchmark’s mix to that uniform one. The split is recomputed with pairs (i, j) of a cell weighted by wi wj , so a gain                  1769
         constant within a cell still has a case-level part of exactly zero and unit weights give the estimator of Sec. 3; the within-               1770
         cell AUC is recomputed with the same comparison weights. Temperatures are those fitted at the benchmark’s mix, on the                       1771
         calibration halves of Secs. 5 and 6, each study on its own temperature grid; they are not refitted under the shift. Intervals are a         1772
         delete-a-group jackknife over images (100 groups). An image bootstrap biases the case-level part towards zero here, because                 1773
         a resampled image’s same-label pairs have kernel zero and receive weight E[w2 ] = 2. At the uniform end the weights are                     1774
         very unequal: the Kish effective sample size of the audit half falls from 92,027 to 7,296.                                                  1775
             Table 30 gives the result for the eight comparisons of the two audits. The group-level part changes sign in seven of them; in           1776

         reverse too: the CLIP model, last on the benchmark by every aggregate, beats geometry by +0.02981 under uniform labels.                     1778

         Q.11. Zero-shot CLIP on the canonical split                                                                                             1947

         The CLIP model of Sec. 6 trains a head on Visual Genome labels. As a first look at a vision–language model used with                    1948
         no training at all, we score the canonical test relations of Sec. 5 zero-shot: frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 [34] encodes the union              1949
         box of subject and object and is compared with one prompt per predicate, “a photo of a {subject} {predicate} a {object}”                1950
         (colab clip zeroshot.py). The map from dataset index to image file follows the codebase’s loader, and every image’s                     1951
         size matches the one stored with the audit. Nothing is trained, so the model has no predicate prior of its own. FREQ is the             1952
         MOTIFS frequency branch, constant within a cell up to 31 relations whose scored pair is a duplicate box with another label              1953
         (hence its AUC of 0.5002). Against FREQ a model’s case-level part is its own within-cell information. “Zero-shot CLIP                   1954
         + FREQ” multiplies the two distributions and renormalises, which leaves the within-cell AUC unchanged. Protocol as in                   1955
         Sec. 5: per-model temperature on the calibration half, split on the other.                                                              1956
             Zero-shot CLIP ranks the correct predicate first for 2.5% of relations, and its group-level loss against FREQ is −0.51900.          1957
         It does carry within-cell information: its AUC is 0.5164, above the 0.5 of any model constant within cells, and its case-level          1958
         part over FREQ is +0.00046, interval [+0.00041, +0.00051]. But this is a small fraction of what MOTIFS learns from                      1959
         Visual Genome: MOTIFS’s AUC is 0.5705, the difference −0.0542, interval [−0.0655, −0.0423], and its case-level part                     1960
         over FREQ is +0.02943. Given the training prior, zero-shot CLIP reaches 0.6289 top-1, but its case-level part over FREQ                 1961
         (+0.00989) is a third of MOTIFS’s. Frozen ViT-B/32 with one prompt per predicate is a weak baseline, at 2.5% top-1 close                1962
         to chance. A larger model with prompt ensembles (below) changes the picture little, but region-level features or fine-tuning            1963
         may carry more, and we draw no general conclusion about vision–language models from it. What it shows is that the split                 1964
         separates a model with no predicate prior and little within-pair information from one with both, in line with evidence that             1965
         such models often treat captions as bags of words [52]. TDE’s case-level part over FREQ, +0.02937, equals MOTIFS’s to                   1966
         the third decimal, as the null of Sec. 5 implies.                                                                                       1967
